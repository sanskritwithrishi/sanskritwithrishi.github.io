    How to use the story trainer

    Each story pair goes with one lesson (Story pair 1 = Lesson 21). Each pair has two stories, and each story is split into sentences, one per line.

    1. Choose a story pair from the menu at the top and type in the password your teacher has given you. You only need to do this once on each device.
    2. Choose Story 1 or Story 2, then pick a sentence from the list.
    3. Translate: tap the Sanskrit words in the order they come in English. If you are right, the meaning is added to your translation. If not, the app tells you what to look for.

    When you’re stuck:

    1. 💡 Hint tells you what kind of word to look for next. Press it again to highlight the word.
    2. 📖 Meaning: press it, then tap any word to see its dictionary form and meaning.
    3. 🔍 Parse: press it, then tap any word to see what kind of word it is and its case or tense.
    4. Transliteration (top right) shows each word in Roman letters.

    Sandhi: stories that are not yet in sandhi have a Sandhi tab. Tap the gold ? between two words, work out how they are written in sandhi, then press Show options. Stories that are already in sandhi have a Split the sandhi tab instead: tap a word with a gold dashed border (✂) and choose the words it is made of. Open Sandhi rules to revise the rules.

    Try to use as few hints as you can. When you finish a sentence, the app tells you how many mistakes and hints you needed.
