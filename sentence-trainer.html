    How to use the trainer

    This app trains you to translate a Sanskrit sentence into English one word at a time, in the order the words come in English.

    1. Choose a set from the menu at the top, then pick a sentence from the list.
    2. Think first. Ask yourself which word should come first in English, which is usually who is doing the action. Work out its meaning in your head.
    3. Tap that word. If it is the right one, its meaning appears under it and is added to your English translation. If it isn't, the app tells you what to look for.
    4. Keep going until the whole sentence is translated.

    When you’re stuck:

    1. 💡 Hint tells you what kind of word to look for next. Press it again to highlight the word.
    2. 📖 Meaning: press it, then tap any word to see its dictionary form and meaning. From there you can press Parse this word for more detail.
    3. 🔍 Parse: press it, then tap any word to see what kind of word it is, its case or tense, and its dictionary form.
    4. Transliteration (top right) shows each word in Roman letters.

    ② Sandhi: once you have translated a sentence, switch to the Sandhi tab. Tap the gold ? between two words, work out in your head how the pair is written in sandhi, then press Show options and choose the right one. Only the rules you have learnt so far are tested. Grey dotted gaps use rules you haven’t met yet, so those words stay as they are. Open New sandhi rules for this set at the bottom to revise the rules.

    Try to use as few hints as you can. When you finish, the app tells you how many mistakes and hints you needed.
