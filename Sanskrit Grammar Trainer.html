How to use the trainer

This app trains you to translate the exercise sentences from Lessons 1–8 into English one word at a time, in the order the words come in English.

  1. Choose a lesson from the menu at the top, then pick a sentence from the list. Sentences are grouped by exercise.
  2. Think first. Ask yourself which word should come first in English, which is usually who is doing the action. Work out its meaning in your head.
  3. Tap that word. If it is the right one, its meaning appears under it and is added to your English translation. If it isn’t, the app tells you what to look for.
  4. Keep going until the whole sentence is translated.

When you’re stuck:

  1. 💡 Hint tells you what kind of word to look for next. Press it again to highlight the word.
  2. 📖 Meaning: press it, then tap any word to see its dictionary form and meaning.
  3. 🔍 Parse: press it, then tap any word to see what kind of word it is, its case or tense, and its dictionary form.
  4. Grammar for this lesson, under the sentence, has the endings and rules you need.
  5. Transliteration (top right) shows each word in Roman letters.

② Vocabulary: Learn shows the lesson’s words; tap a card to see its English meaning. Test yourself gives you each Sanskrit word and four English meanings to choose from.

Try to use as few hints as you can. When you finish a sentence, the app tells you how many mistakes and hints you needed.
